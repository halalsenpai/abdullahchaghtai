- Architected and developed a scalable multi-tenant restaurant management platform enabling restaurants to manage their online presence, menus, and reservations

- Implemented a headless CMS system allowing restaurant owners to easily manage their content, menus, and pricing in real-time without technical knowledge

- Built a sophisticated booking system with real-time availability checking, automated confirmation emails, and integration with restaurant POS systems

- Integrated Stripe payment processing for secure handling of deposits and pre-payments for reservations

- Developed a responsive, mobile-first design providing an optimal user experience across all devices

- Implemented role-based access control (RBAC) for different user types (admin, staff, customers) with appropriate permissions

- Created a multi-tenant architecture allowing the platform to serve multiple restaurants while maintaining data isolation

- Utilized AWS services for scalable hosting, image storage, and email delivery

- Achieved 40% reduction in reservation management time for restaurant staff through automation
